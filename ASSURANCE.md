# Assurance boundaries

The exact slack identity and the attainment arguments are analytic proofs. Exact
rational replay checks the constructed trajectories, gap formulas and certificate
identities over stated finite parameter grids in ordinary and optimized Python.
Negative controls are required to raise explicit verification exceptions.

The theorem concerns arbitrary admissible comparison pairs. It does not establish
the same sharp coefficient when the comparison must be a saddle pair, a
last-iterate residual, an objective error, a lower bound for other algorithms, or
a uniformly conditioned smooth strongly convex class. At `alpha L = 2`, general
attainment is unresolved. Finite replay does not prove convex interpolation,
novelty or external acceptance. AI review is not independent human peer review.
