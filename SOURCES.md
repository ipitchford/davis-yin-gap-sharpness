# Source inventory

The manuscript bibliography is authoritative. The load-bearing external source is
Nguyen et al. (arXiv:2506.23475), whose stated Davis–Yin ordering and step-size-one
conjecture are the direct target. Pedregosa's exposition and Pedregosa–Gidel are
used for finite-horizon metric/index comparisons. Davis–Yin supplies the base
algorithm. Taylor–Hendrickx–Glineur and Ryu et al. provide interpolation and
performance-estimation context.

Third-party papers are cited, not redistributed. The exact certificate, explicit
families, attainment proof and endpoint extension are supplied in full here.
